Our approach combines practical education with creative exploration and career preparation.

To help learners develop practical creative and technology skills that can support their professional ambitions.

FAQ

We’re here to help.

What courses does Prism Multimedia offer?

Programs include Graphic Design, Motion Graphics, UI Design & Development, Digital Marketing, UX Design, 2D Animation, 3D Animation, Audio & Video Editing, Visual Effects, EDP and PGDIM.

Are courses suitable for beginners?

Yes. Programs are structured for beginners as well as working professionals, with practical assignments and portfolio-focused learning.

Do you guarantee placements?

No. We provide career support such as portfolio guidance, interview preparation and opportunity exploration — without guaranteed placement claims.

How can I enroll?

Enquire through this website, call admissions, or visit our Ameerpet campus. Counselors will guide you on batches and next steps.
