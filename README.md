About Prism Multimedia

Prism Multimedia is an organization established in 1999 to provide professional multimedia education and skill development. With years of experience in training and development, the institute prepares students for careers in Graphic Design, Animation, VFX, UI/UX Design, Digital Marketing, Video Editing, and related creative industries.

The institute also conducts customized corporate training programs and focuses on practical learning that matches current industry requirements.
